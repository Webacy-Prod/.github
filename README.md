## Overview

**Webacy** is an application-layer safety platform dedicated to protecting users against scams, hacks, and mistakes across the blockchain. Our suite of tools and APIs empowers wallets, decentralized exchanges (DEXs), protocols, and applications to enhance security and provide a safer Web3 experience.

## Developer Portal

To integrate Webacy's safety features into your platform:
